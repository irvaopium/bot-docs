Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Our Discord bot may collect minimal data necessary for functionality, such as:

  • Discord User IDs (to identify who executes commands)
  • Server/Guild IDs (to manage server-specific configurations)
  • Command usage data (for operational and debugging purposes)

2. How We Use Data

The collected data is strictly used to deliver bot commands and maintain functionality. We do not store private message contents or personal identifying information outside of standard Discord parameters.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or trade any collected data with third parties under any circumstances.

4. Data Retention and Removal

Data is stored only as long as necessary to provide the service. Users can request the removal of any stored data by contacting the bot administrator directly.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via Discord to the server administrator or bot owner.
